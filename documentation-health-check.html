Documentation Health Check

Find the structural problems that keep documentation expensive.

A focused assessment for software teams dealing with legacy Help systems, maintenance overload, duplicated content, brittle publishing, weak ownership, or unclear modernization priorities.

Start a conversationSee the scope

Some documentation projects do not need more content. They need a clearer architecture, a healthier workflow, and fewer hidden maintenance costs.

Assessment scope

Eight places where documentation systems quietly accumulate debt.

The assessment can be tailored to MadCap Flare environments or applied more broadly to enterprise documentation operations.

01

Information architecture

Navigation, hierarchy, findability, content types, topic boundaries, and audience paths.

02

Content reuse

Duplicated material, snippet strategy, variables, conditions, single sourcing, and reuse overhead.

03

Publishing

Targets, outputs, CSS, templates, build reliability, PDF production, and release delivery.

04

Repository health

Git structure, branching, ownership, review patterns, backup, and change visibility.

05

Workflow

How documentation moves from product planning through authoring, review, release, and maintenance.

06

AI readiness

Safe opportunities for research, impact analysis, first drafts, consistency checks, and automation.

07

Search & analytics

Search behavior, failed queries, usage patterns, feedback loops, and measurable content decisions.

08

Governance

Standards, terminology, ownership, review expectations, lifecycle rules, and documentation debt.

What you receive

A practical modernization map, not a ceremonial audit.

  • Current-state findings and risk areas
  • Prioritized recommendations by effort and impact
  • Quick wins that can reduce maintenance immediately
  • Longer-term architecture and workflow recommendations
  • Suggested AI, Git, analytics, and publishing opportunities
  • A roadmap teams can actually use after the assessment ends

Good fit

This assessment is especially useful when...

Your Help system has grown for years without a structural review.

Writers spend more time maintaining the system than improving the customer experience.

Content is duplicated across products, outputs, or audiences.

One person understands the publishing process and everyone hopes they never leave.

Your team wants to use AI but does not yet have a safe, useful workflow.

You know the documentation needs modernization, but not where to begin.
