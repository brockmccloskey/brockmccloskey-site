Brock Stories

Short stories that explain how I think and work. These are the raw materials for interviews, project pages, and future case studies.

  1. The 100 Snippets Story. At NextAxiom, I redesigned widget property documentation using nearly 100 reusable Flare snippets so users saw relevant information in context and writers could maintain shared information from one place.
  2. The Equinix Platform Story. At Equinix, my role expanded from Flare consultant to documentation platform owner supporting publishing, GitHub, localization, staging, production, and a growing team of writers.
  3. The Trintech AI Workflow Story. At Trintech, I began integrating Codex with Azure DevOps documentation workflows to support release notes, documentation impact analysis, and affected-topic identification.
  4. The First Technical Writing Job Story. A career counselor connected my love of computers and writing to a profession I had never heard of. After being rejected for lacking FrameMaker experience, I took training, reapplied, and got the job.
  5. The Systems, Not Jigsaw Puzzles Story. When I say I like puzzles, I do not mean a 5,000-piece jigsaw puzzle on the kitchen table. I mean real systems: products, workflows, maps, publishing processes, documentation architecture, and the question of how the pieces fit together.
